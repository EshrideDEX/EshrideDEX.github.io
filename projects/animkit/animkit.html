AnimKit

Download

AnimKit is a collection of tools designed to make animation workflows faster and more convenient.

AnimKit has a dedicated Bone Dock for editing the transforms of multiple bones at once including euler rotation editing.

Hold SHIFT to select multiple bones from the Bone List.

Copy and paste the transforms from one bone to another.

Need to mirror transforms to the other side?

Hold SHIFT before clicking the paste button to paste flipped transforms.

Open the Pose Library tab at the bottom of the editor to start saving and for your rigs to use later!

Use the Pose Mirror Pose button in the Bone Dock to mirror the entire pose of your rig to the other side.

FAQ

What is AnimKit?

AnimKit is a Godot addon that provides a collection of tools to make working with bones, rigs, and animations easier.

What versions/platforms does it work with?

AnimKit works on every platform supported by Godot, as it is written entirely in GDScript and does not use any compiled binaries. It has been tested with Godot 4.3–4.7 stable stable.

Is AnimKit free?

For now, yes. AnimKit is free and open source. However, as the project grows, it may eventually have a one-time purchase fee.

Where can I get it?

You can download the latest release from the bottom of this page, or you can install it directly in your project through the Godot Asset Library tab in the Godot Editor.

How do I install AnimKit?
  1. Copy the anim_kit/ folder into your Godot project's addons/ folder
  2. Enable the plugin in Project → Project Settings → Plugins
Where do I report problems or ask for help?

You can open a new issue over on GitHub.
